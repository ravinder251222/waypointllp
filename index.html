    Process

    How we deliver

    A clear path from the first conversation to a system your team can run and maintain.

    1. Step 01

      Discover

      We study the machine or process, existing hardware, constraints and production goals — on site where needed.

    2. Step 02

      Design

      Control architecture, I/O lists, product selection, panel layouts and screen flow, agreed before build.

    3. Step 03

      Build

      Panel assembly, PLC and HMI programming, drive parameterisation and SCADA configuration.

    4. Step 04

      Commission

      On-site testing, I/O and loop checks, tuning and handover with your operations team.

    5. Step 05

      Support

      Troubleshooting, modifications, spares and training so the system keeps performing.

        Who it is for

        Built for learners who want to work with real automation systems

        Whether you are starting a career or strengthening skills on the job, sessions focus on what you will actually do in front of a panel, a drive or a SCADA screen.

        Students

        Learners preparing for roles in automation, maintenance and industrial engineering who want practical, job-relevant skills.

        Working professionals

        Engineers and technicians who want to program, configure and troubleshoot automation equipment with more confidence.

        How we teach

        A hands-on approach, from concept to troubleshooting

        1. Concept to configuration

          Every topic moves from how it works to how you set it up, step by step.

        2. Application examples

          Logic, screens and parameters built around typical machine and process scenarios.

        3. Troubleshooting mindset

          Learn to read alarms and status, diagnose faults and verify that a fix really works.

        • Configure VFDs and servo systems for a given application
        • Approach breakdowns with a structured troubleshooting method
